## Color Palette

Navy: #091124
Soft Navy: #0A162E
Gold: #D4AF37
Ivory: #F5F0E8
Linen: #E5E0D8
Warm Wood: #C9B39A

## Typography

Headings:
Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, or Libre Baskerville

Body:
Inter, Montserrat, Lato, or Open Sans

## Design Reminder

Keep the Canva designs clean and architectural. Use fewer words, larger images, strong spacing, navy backgrounds, gold accents, and readable ivory text.
